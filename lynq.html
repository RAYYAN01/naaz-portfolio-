Why it exists

Leads go cold in 48 hours. Your competitor just replies faster.

Not because they are better. Because someone answered at 9pm and you answered at 11am. Every business we work with is losing revenue in that gap — and no amount of team discipline closes it, because the system requires a human to be awake.

How it works

Lead in. Warm conversation out.

  1. 01

    A lead messages your WhatsApp, your website widget, or clicks a WhatsApp ad.

  2. 02

    Lynq replies instantly, answering from your brochures, price lists, and FAQs.

  3. 03

    It captures name, phone, budget, and requirement — into your CRM, no typing.

  4. 04

    If they want to book, it checks your Google Calendar and confirms the slot.

  5. 05

    For outbound, it dials your lead list on a schedule and qualifies each contact.

  6. 06

    Every lead is scored Hot, Warm, or Cold in real time from actual behaviour.

  7. 07

    The moment a lead is hot, your agent gets an alert — and a documented history.
