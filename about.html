Who we are

Naaz AI Labs is an artificial intelligence research and development studio based in JP Nagar, Bengaluru. We build websites, CRMs, and automation for businesses that have outgrown the tools they started with.

We started because the same thing kept happening: a business would pay for a website, get something that looked acceptable, and discover six months later that it did nothing. No leads captured. No follow-up. No data. A brochure with a contact form.

So we build the other thing. The system underneath — the one that answers at 2am, scores the lead, books the call, and tells you which channel actually earns its budget.

That work turned into a product. Lynq is our AI automation platform — a full AI workforce on WhatsApp, web chat, and phone, live on a client's own number in seven days.

How we work

Four commitments we do not negotiate.

01

Business first, technology second

We will not recommend AI where a form would do. The stack follows the commercial problem, never the other way around.

02

One team, start to finish

The people who scoped it build it. Nothing gets lost in a handoff between a design agency and an offshore dev shop.

03

Built to be handed over

Documented, standard, and yours. We build so that another team could take it over — which is exactly why most clients never need to.

04

Measured, or it didn't happen

Every engagement ships with the instrumentation to prove whether it worked. We would rather know than assume.
