Your side of it

We need about four hours of your time.

Total, across the whole engagement. Two hours in discovery, one at wireframe sign-off, one at pre-launch review. Everything else is our problem, reported to you weekly in writing.

Week 0Discovery session
Two hours. Your business, your numbers, your constraints. We do the rest of the analysis without you.
Gate 1Wireframe sign-off
One hour. The last cheap moment to change the shape of the product. We make it count.
Gate 2Pre-launch review
One hour on the real system, with real data, before anyone outside sees it.
OngoingWeekly written update
What shipped, what's next, what's at risk. Read it in two minutes or ignore it — the environment is always live.
